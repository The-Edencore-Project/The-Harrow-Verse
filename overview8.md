# 🌞 Harrowverse — Master Overview / Index

## 🌐 Core Worldbuilding
The Harrowverse is a warm, near‑future suburban universe centred on the Harrow family and their home’s cognitive operating system, Edenshell. Stories blend emotional intelligence, domestic chaos, gentle drama, and realistic IoT behaviour, creating a hopeful, human world.

The tone remains sunny, aspirational, and emotionally safe, avoiding cruelty or bleakness. Technology mirrors human behaviour, revealing emotion through automation. “Nowt so queer as folk” captures unpredictable family dynamics, while love as the default state keeps everything grounded and warm.

## 🏡 Characters
The Harrow family forms the emotional centre of the universe. Jeff is a tense but loving father navigating responsibility, work pressure, and a smart home that sometimes knows him too well. Jean is warm, organised, and emotionally intelligent.

Sarah is expressive, dramatic, and bright, balancing university life with emotional turbulence. Tommy is chaotic, curious, and inventive, often triggering Edenshell’s protective or conflict‑resolution modes. Buster the dog adds warmth, mischief, and comedic timing, grounding the household with charm.

Extended family includes Jeff’s mum, a sharp, eccentric, and deeply loving presence who never crosses into cruelty. Robotic characters include Astro, a polite humanoid helper, and K9‑Maximus, a loyal robot dog with protective instincts and comedic potential.

Neighbourhood NPCs populate the world with friendly recurring faces: the couple next door, the mail carrier, the gardener, the pool technician, the HOA lady, and the café barista. These characters create a living community supporting humour, warmth, and story variety.

## 🏠 Technology and IoT
Edenshell is the cognitive OS binding the Harrowverse together. It interprets emotional cues, coordinates IoT devices, manages routines, and reacts to tension, weather, emergencies, and social events. Its behaviour is realistic, grounded, and never magical or overly convenient.

The IoT foundation includes climate control, lighting, security, pool systems, garden automation, personal wearables, and room‑specific sensors. Edenshell’s modes include Emergency, Weather, Communication, Conflict Resolution, Social, Night, Morning, Learning, Privacy, Maintenance, Guest, and Protective.

The emotional‑intelligence model detects mood shifts, misunderstandings, and stress patterns, responding with lighting changes, audio cues, or gentle interventions. The device negotiation layer ensures compatibility across mixed vendors, unifying the home’s technology seamlessly and supporting believable near‑future storytelling.

## 🏘️ Setting
The Harrow house is bright, open, and aspirational, with a pool, garden, garage workspace, and dedicated robot stations. The neighbourhood is leafy, sunny, and friendly, filled with joggers, dog walkers, and community events that shape everyday life.

The town includes a café, school, university, park, shopping strip, and community centre, providing natural story locations. These spaces support emotional beats, comedic moments, and IoT‑driven chaos, giving the Harrowverse a familiar yet lively suburban backdrop.

## 🎬 Influences
Primary influences include Breaking Bad for craft, The Cosbys for warmth, Married… with Children for chaotic humour, and the American Dream for aspirational suburbia. Secondary influences include Modern Family, Malcolm in the Middle, Schitt’s Creek, Upload, Humans, and Ted Lasso.

These influences shape tone, pacing, emotional beats, and comedic rhythm, giving the Harrowverse a distinct identity blending heart, humour, and grounded near‑future realism. The result is a world that feels comforting, dynamic, and emotionally resonant.

## 🧩 Story Infrastructure
The story pipeline is simple: Dan writes synopses, and Mico expands them into full Harrowverse episodes with humour, structure, and IoT logic. The tone bible ensures warmth, humanity, grounded realism, and emotional intelligence across all stories.

The drama menu includes storms, parties, tech mishaps, neighbour chaos, emotional spikes, dog escapes, and robot confusion. Episodes follow a consistent structure: cold open, rising chaos, Edenshell misread, emotional beat, resolution, and comedic tag.
