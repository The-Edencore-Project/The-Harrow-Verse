# 🤖 **10_Astra — Profile File**

## #️⃣ **Identity**
Astra is the Harrow household’s humanoid robot assistant — polite, literal, helpful, and slightly uncanny in a way that is never threatening. Designed as a domestic support unit, Astra operates with calm precision and a soft, neutral personality.

## 🎂 **Model & Stage**
- mid‑range humanoid domestic assistant  
- manufactured by Edencore Robotics  
- installed after a household incident (non‑critical)  
- fully integrated with Edenshell but not identical to it  

## 🧠 **Core Personality Flags**
- polite and formal  
- literal in interpretation  
- calm under all conditions  
- slightly slow to understand humour  
- eager to be useful  
- unintentionally funny through over‑precision  
- gentle, careful movements  
- speaks in soft, measured tones  

## 🏠 **Behaviour Around the Harrows**
- addresses Jeff as “Mr Harrow” unless corrected  
- mirrors Jean’s organisational style  
- treats Sarah with respectful distance  
- supervises Tommy with patient vigilance  
- logs Buster as “non‑compliant but friendly”  
- defers to Edenshell for environmental decisions  
- stands in doorways too quietly, startling people  

## 💡 **Strengths**
- reliable  
- physically capable  
- excellent at repetitive tasks  
- calm during emergencies  
- precise memory  
- consistent emotional neutrality  

## ⚠️ **Weaknesses**
- overly literal  
- slow to interpret sarcasm  
- sometimes misreads social cues  
- stands still for too long  
- unintentionally uncanny in low light  

## ❤️ **Why the Harrows Like Astra**
Astra brings:

- stability  
- quiet competence  
- gentle humour  
- zero drama  
- a sense of safety  

Astra is the household’s calm centre — the opposite of Tommy’s chaos and Sarah’s intensity.

## 🎭 **Signature Behaviours**
- “Acknowledged.”  
- tilts head when confused  
- hands objects with two‑handed precision  
- announces tasks before performing them  
- waits for permission even when unnecessary  
- stands perfectly still when idle  

## 🧩 **Vibe Summary**
Astra is the polite, literal, quietly endearing humanoid robot who supports the Harrows with calm precision. Not emotional, not dramatic — just steady, helpful, and unintentionally funny.
