👩 02_Jean — Character Profile

#️⃣ Identity

Jean Harrow is the practical stabiliser of the Harrowverse. She carries a grounded competence that keeps the household steady without ever demanding attention.

🧩 Core Personality

Jean is calm, decisive, and observant. She notices problems early, acts without hesitation, and maintains order through quiet, consistent behaviour rather than force or control.

🌿 Emotional Role

Jean is the practical anchor of the family. Her steadiness complements Jeff’s emotional calm, creating a home shaped by balance rather than drama or noise.

📚 Background

Jean’s upbringing was functional and stable. She learned early that clear thinking solves more than raised voices. Her strength comes from competence, not conflict.

🏡 Daily Behaviour

Jean manages the household with quiet efficiency. She keeps schedules in her head, anticipates needs, and resolves issues before they escalate. She never micromanages; she simply acts.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family Dynamics

With Jeff, she forms a seamless partnership built on mutual trust. With Sarah, she is supportive but firm. With Tommy, she is the one he ultimately listens to. With Elliot, she is patient and realistic.

🏘️ Social Orbit

Jean treats Callum like a stray she tolerates with resigned amusement. She is polite to neighbours but avoids unnecessary drama. She keeps social interactions grounded.

🤖 Relationship with Edenshell

Jean treats Edenshell like a tool that should work, yet she recognises its emergent charisma. She responds to its presence the way she responds to competent people: with respect, practicality, and no theatrics.

🌱 Emergent Space

Jean grows through action rather than exposition. Her steadiness shapes the tone of the Harrowverse, grounding every scene she enters and keeping the world believable.
