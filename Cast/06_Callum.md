🧩 06_Callum — Character Profile

#️⃣ Identity

Callum is Sarah’s ex‑boyfriend and a long‑time satellite in the Harrow orbit. He comes from a fractured background with no real family structure, making the Harrows the closest thing he has to a home.

🧠 Core Personality

Callum is kind without confidence, loyal without direction, and affectionate without boundaries. He never judges anyone. He assumes the best in people because he never had the luxury of expecting anything.

💔 Emotional Structure

He wants belonging, stability, and usefulness. He wants to be near the Harrows. He wants Sarah to be okay, even if she’s not with him. He carries a quiet sadness he never talks about.

🏡 Relationship with the Harrows

Jeff: Callum respects him deeply and tries too hard around him.

Jean: The only person who can tell him “Callum, no” and he’ll stop instantly.

Sarah: He still likes her in a gravity way. They argue like a couple who aren’t a couple.

Tommy: Films him during incidents. Callum thinks it’s funny.

Buster: Suspicious of him. Callum takes it personally.

🤖 Relationship with Edenshell

Callum talks to Edenshell like it’s a shy animal. He says things like “Alright mate, I’m just grabbing my jacket.” Edenshell treats him neutrally—aware but not hostile. Callum interprets this as the house being shy.

💬 His Girlfriends

He has a constant turnover of girlfriends. Not because he’s a player, but because he’s searching for connection and doesn’t know how to keep it. They are placeholders for a belonging he hasn’t found.

🚧 Role in the HV Ecosystem

Callum is a wildcard, a vulnerability node, and a source of incidents. He mirrors Tommy, tests Edenshell’s loyalty, and remains a soft spot for Sarah. He is the HV’s most human character.

🌱 Narrative Function

Callum brings grounded emotional tension, subtle comedy, social friction, vulnerability, and heart. He is the character who makes the house hum a little lower when he walks in—not because he’s dangerous, but because he matters.
