👦 04_Tommy — Character Profile

#️⃣ Identity

Tommy Harrow is the household’s walking entropy generator. Curious, impulsive, and tech‑native, he creates accidental chaos without malice and protects his family’s privacy with fierce loyalty.

🎧 Core Personality

Tommy lives half in the real world and half in his headphones. He notices odd details, presses buttons too quickly, and learns through consequences. His chaos is sincere, never cruel.

📱 Filming Instinct

Tommy films the orbiters, not the family. Outsiders are fair game; robots and Buster are constant content. He only films Sarah during her colourful, theatrical moments—never private ones—and he never posts those clips.

🧠 Behavioural Notes

Tommy leaves gates open, forgets steps, and starts projects he doesn’t finish. He apologises quickly, means well, and unintentionally triggers half of Edenshell’s emergent behaviours.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family Dynamics

With Jeff, he relaxes. With Jean, he tries to behave. With Sarah, he alternates between annoyance and loyalty. With Elliot, he becomes more chaotic. With Callum, he asks too many questions.

🤖 Relationship with Edenshell

Tommy treats Edenshell like a background character with charisma. He talks to it casually, films its oddities, and becomes the primary source of data for its emergent responses.

🌱 Emergent Space

Tommy’s arc is about understanding his impact. His curiosity drives the story forward, his mistakes create narrative openings, and his loyalty keeps the Harrowverse grounded.
