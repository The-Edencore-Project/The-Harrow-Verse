👧 03_Sarah — Character Profile

#️⃣ Identity

Sarah Harrow is the emotional pulse of the Harrowverse. She feels everything fully, reacts immediately, and brings colour and movement into a household built on calm foundations.

🌦️ Core Personality

Sarah responds with whatever emotion rises first. She talks fast, storms in, goes quiet, paces, or catastrophises. Her intensity is honesty, not chaos, shaped by context.

🎓 Background

Sarah is university‑age, bright, capable, and constantly overthinking her own life. She moves between home and campus, balancing independence with the need for stability.

🧠 Emotional Role

Sarah is the weather system of the Harrowverse. She doesn’t create drama for attention; she reacts to life with immediacy. Her feelings shift quickly but sincerely.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family Dynamics

With Jeff, she softens. With Jean, she pushes and returns. With Tommy, she alternates between rivalry and loyalty. With Elliot, she enjoys emotional ease. With Callum, history lingers.

🤖 Relationship with Edenshell

Sarah vents to Edenshell because it listens without judgement. Its emergent charisma makes it a safe presence, a quiet witness she trusts during emotional overload.

💬 Behavioural Notes

Sarah paces when anxious, storms when frustrated, and collapses into silence when overwhelmed. She apologises quickly when she realises she’s gone too far.

🌱 Emergent Space

Sarah grows through moments: late‑night kitchen talks, quiet drives, arguments, reconciliations, and unresolved threads. Her arc is about clarity, not calmness.
