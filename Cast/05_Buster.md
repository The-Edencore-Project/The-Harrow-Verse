🐕 05_Buster — Character Profile

#️⃣ Identity

Buster Harrow is the household’s most grounded and chaotic presence. He is instinctive, stubborn, food‑driven, and emotionally unbothered by human complexity. He is not a mascot; he is a behavioural constant.

🧠 Core Personality

Buster is busy, not naughty. He follows scent, sound, and curiosity with total commitment. He causes trouble by accident and solves problems by accident. He respects Jeff, tolerates Jean, judges Sarah, and opportunistically partners with Tommy.

🐾 Behavioural Patterns

Buster escapes through gaps because the world is interesting. He returns home because the Harrows are his anchor. He sleeps in strange places, stares at people like he’s assessing their soul, and eats things he shouldn’t because he is a dog.

📹 Relationship with Tommy

Tommy does not constantly film Buster. He films incidents—moments with energy, weirdness, or stakes. Buster escaping, confronting neighbours, reacting to Edenshell, or committing food‑related crimes. Tommy films events, not daily life.

🏡 Family Dynamics

With Jeff, Buster shares quiet mutual respect. With Jean, he behaves mostly. With Sarah, he is a calm mirror. With Tommy, he is a co‑conspirator. With Elliot, he is indifferent. With Callum, he is suspicious.

🤖 Relationship with Edenshell

Edenshell tracks Buster like a parent tracks a toddler—aware but not controlling. It adjusts the environment around him. Buster treats Edenshell like a large, silent animal in the walls. He respects it, listens to it, and occasionally growls at it.

🌱 Emergent Space

Buster’s arc is about presence, not growth. He anchors scenes, breaks tension, creates tension, and reveals character through how people respond to him. He is the Harrow‑Verse’s living barometer.
