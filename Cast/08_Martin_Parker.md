# 🧑‍🔧 **08_Martin Parker — Profile File**

## #️⃣ **Identity**
Martin Parker is the male half of the Parker duo — the polite, slightly clingy, overly enthusiastic neighbour who orbits the Harrow family with gentle devotion. He is harmless, eager, and socially awkward in a way that is endearing rather than intrusive.

## 🎂 **Age & Stage**
- mid‑40s  
- married to Denise Parker  
- long‑term resident of the street  
- works a stable but unexciting job (non‑plot‑critical)

## 🧠 **Core Personality Flags**
- overly friendly  
- slightly clingy  
- eager to be included  
- socially awkward but well‑meaning  
- deferential to Jeff  
- impressed by everything the Harrows do  
- nervous around conflict  
- enthusiastic about mundane things  
- unintentionally funny through earnestness  

## 🏠 **Behaviour Around the Harrows**
- waves too early and too long  
- walks over slowly, waiting for an invitation  
- compliments Jean’s cooking excessively  
- laughs at Tommy’s antics even when he doesn’t get the joke  
- treats Callum like a local celebrity  
- speaks to Edenshell as if it’s a person  
- leaves early because he “doesn’t want to be a bother”  
- checks with Denise before saying anything remotely bold  

## 💡 **Strengths**
- kind  
- reliable  
- neighbourly  
- emotionally perceptive in a soft, timid way  
- respectful of boundaries even when hovering near them  

## ⚠️ **Weaknesses**
- clingy  
- overly apologetic  
- easily flustered  
- tries too hard to be liked  
- unintentionally awkward  
- overthinks invitations and social cues  

## ❤️ **Why the Harrows Like Him**
He is:

- harmless  
- warm  
- supportive  
- unintentionally funny  
- deeply respectful of their space  
- the kind of neighbour who means well even when he’s a bit much  

He adds gentle comedy and community texture to the Harrowverse.

## 🎭 **Signature Behaviours**
- “Oh! We were just… uh… heading inside, but if you need anything—anything at all—just shout.”  
- stands at the edge of the driveway waiting to be noticed  
- brings small, unnecessary gifts (biscuits, candles, seasonal napkins)  
- whispers to Denise about Harrow household chaos  
- peeks through curtains but only out of concern, not nosiness  

## 🧩 **Vibe Summary**
Martin is the neighbour who hovers, lingers, and hopes to be included — but never pushes.  
Soft, clingy, polite, and unintentionally comedic.  
A perfect middle‑orbit presence in the Harrowverse.
