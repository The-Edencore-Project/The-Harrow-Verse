🧑‍🎓 07_Elliot — Profile File

#️⃣ Identity

Elliot is Sarah’s current boyfriend, defined by his gentle temperament, academic focus, and socially anxious tendencies. He is the softest presence in the cast and functions as the Harrowverse’s primary “outsider lens” on the Harrow household.

🎓 Age & Stage

20–21 years old

Second‑year university student

Same academic year as Sarah

🧠 Core Personality Flags

agreeable and conflict‑avoidant

apologises frequently

nervous laugh when stressed

over‑explains simple points

easily startled and socially fragile

academically intelligent but timid

eager to please, especially around the Harrows

🏠 Behaviour Around the Harrow Family

intimidated by Jean’s precision

quietly terrified of Jeff’s silence

confused but polite toward Tommy

tries too hard to impress Callum

deeply affectionate toward Sarah

Edenshell categorises him as “low‑resistance behaviour”

💡 Strengths

patient and gentle

reliable and consistent

emotionally safe for Sarah

academically capable

zero drama or volatility

⚠️ Weaknesses

anxious under pressure

timid in social settings

folds quickly in conflict

overthinks every interaction

tries too hard to be liked

❤️ Why Sarah Likes Him

Elliot is the first boy who brings no chaos, no emotional drain, and no competition. He is calm, compliant, and steady — a relief after the intensity of the Harrow household.

📅 Longevity

Elliot is not a temporary fling. He remains a stable, low‑conflict presence in Sarah’s life and integrates into the family dynamic over time.

🧩 Vibe Summary

Elliot is smart enough to be interesting, awkward enough to be funny, gentle enough to be lovable, and harmless enough not to disrupt the Harrow family’s emotional balance.
