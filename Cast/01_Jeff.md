🧔 01_Jeff — Character Profile

#️⃣ Identity

Jeff Harrow is the steady centre of the Harrowverse. A father and husband shaped by military service, he carries quiet gratitude into every ordinary day.

🧩 Core Personality

Jeff is calm, methodical, and emotionally grounded. He processes chaos slowly and deliberately, offering stability without demanding attention. His humour is dry and understated.

🎖️ Background

Jeff’s military past is never theatrical. He avoids dramatic retellings and carries no visible bravado. His experience gives him perspective, making civilian life feel like a gift.

🔧 Daily Behaviour

Jeff maintains routines that keep the household steady. He keeps keys in the same place, fuels the car early, and fixes problems before anyone notices. He never raises his voice.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family Dynamics

With Jean, Jeff forms a quiet partnership built on mutual steadiness. With Sarah, he is gentle; with Tommy, amused; with Elliot, patient. Buster listens to him instinctively.

🏡 Relationship with Edenshell

Jeff treats Edenshell like reliable equipment. He accepts its presence without awe or irritation, responding to its deadpan moments with simple acknowledgement.

🌱 Emergent Space

Jeff grows through behaviour rather than exposition. His character remains open, defined by actions, reactions, and the grounded realism of everyday life.
