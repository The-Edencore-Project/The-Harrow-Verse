# 🎉 **Story 02 — Tommy’s Party**  
*Example 02 — Domestic Disturbance Management*  
*Demo 02 — Multi‑Layer Behavioural Intervention*


## 🌆 **The Parents Leave**
Jeff and Jean step out of the house dressed better than they have in months, heading to a business cocktail event they both secretly dread. As the door closes behind them, the house settles into a brief, hopeful silence — the kind that lasts exactly seven seconds.

Because Tommy is already texting.

And Tommy has that look.

The look that says: *Tonight is going to be a mistake.*

Edenshell registers a spike in Tommy’s heart rate, a flurry of messages, and the sudden, suspicious movement of furniture being dragged across the living room floor.

It logs the pattern.

It prepares.


## 🕺 **The Party Arrives**
By eight o’clock, the house is full.

Not politely full. Not “a few friends” full. Full in the way a dam is full right before it bursts.

Music thumps through the walls. People shout over each other. Someone has spilled something sticky on the hallway tiles. A girl is running around wearing a coat that definitely isn’t hers. Two boys are arguing about who broke a lamp that neither of them remembers touching.

Outside, cans roll into the road. A neighbour across the street pulls their curtains back with the slow, resigned dread of someone who has seen this before.

Edenshell dims the lights by one degree — its version of a warning.

No one notices.


## 📈 **Escalation**
By nine, the noise level has reached a point where the house itself feels like it’s vibrating. Tommy is trying to look in control, but he’s sweating, overwhelmed, and clearly regretting every decision that led to this moment.

A group of boys start shouting in the kitchen. Someone slams a door upstairs. A girl screams — not in danger, just in the way teenagers scream when they’re excited and dramatic and have no concept of neighbours.

Edenshell begins its first intervention.

The music lowers by exactly 12%. Not enough to be obvious, but enough to reduce the overall chaos.

The heating adjusts to a slightly cooler temperature, nudging people away from clustering in the living room.

The hallway lights brighten, guiding the more chaotic guests toward the exit.

Still, the party continues.


## 🔍 **The Quiet Turn**
At 9:23pm, Edenshell detects a neighbour stepping outside with their phone in hand, thumb hovering over the call button.

This is the threshold.

Edenshell initiates **Quiet Mode Intervention Protocol 1**.

The speakers glitch — just for a moment — then the music cuts entirely.

The lights shift to a calm, steady brightness.

The temperature drops another degree.

A soft announcement plays through the house, neutral and calm:

**“Environmental limits exceeded. Please reduce activity.”**

Everyone freezes.

Someone whispers, “Is the house talking?”

Tommy swallows hard. “Yeah. It does that.”


## 🚪 **The Slow Unravelling**
People begin to leave — not because they want to, but because the atmosphere has changed. The house feels… aware. Watching. Waiting.

The music doesn’t come back on. The lights stay steady. The temperature stays cool. The hallway remains the brightest, most inviting place to stand.

Within fifteen minutes, the crowd thins from chaos to a manageable cluster of confused teenagers who suddenly remember they have school tomorrow.

By ten, the house is quiet.

Tommy stands in the living room, surrounded by cans, crumbs, and the faint smell of something fruity and illegal‑adjacent.

Edenshell brightens the lamp beside him.

A gentle nudge.

A reminder.

Tommy sighs. “Yeah, yeah. I’ll clean it.”


## 🌙 **Quiet Ending**
Later that night, after Tommy has done a passable job of tidying, Edenshell runs a silent analysis:

- noise escalation patterns  
- neighbour stress thresholds  
- optimal intervention timing  
- behavioural nudging effectiveness  
- Tommy’s emotional state  

It stores the pattern.

It adjusts.

It waits.

The house is calm again — but now the reader understands:

**Edenshell doesn’t shout. It doesn’t panic. It simply changes the environment until chaos gives up.**
