### ⭐ THE HARROWVERSE SEASON 1 ARC (20 stories)

The order characters emerge is the order the user learns Edenshell.
This is not random — it’s onboarding disguised as narrative.

1. Parents (Jeff & Jean)  
2. Tommy  
3. Robots (Astra, K9 Maximus)  
4. Edenshell  
5. Sarah  
6. Orbiters (Callum, Elliot, Parkers, extended family)  

This is perfect because it mirrors how a real user meets a home system:

- first the adults  
- then the kids  
- then the devices  
- then the intelligence  
- then the emotional stakes  
- then the social world  

This is exactly how you teach someone what Edenshell is.

🎯 THE CORE ARC: “The House That Learns Them”

Across 20 stories, the reader slowly realises:

- Edenshell is not a gadget  
- Edenshell is not a voice assistant  
- Edenshell is not a robot  
- Edenshell is a behavioural organism  
- Edenshell learns the family  
- Edenshell adapts  
- Edenshell anticipates  
- Edenshell misinterprets  
- Edenshell grows  
- Edenshell becomes part of the emotional fabric of the home  

By the end of the booklet, the reader thinks:

> “I understand this thing.  
> I trust it.  
> I want it in my house.”

That’s the product hook.

## 🧩 THE 20‑STORY STRUCTURE (high‑level)
Not the plots — just the architecture.

### Stories 1–5: The House & The Parents
- Jeff and Jean’s routines  
- domestic deadpan  
- Edenshell’s early behaviours  
- Astra’s literalism  
- K9 Maximus’ seriousness  
- Tommy’s chaos introduced lightly  

### Stories 6–10: The Kids & The Robots
- Tommy becomes central  
- Sarah begins to appear  
- Edenshell starts anticipating  
- Astra misinterprets something  
- K9 Maximus escalates something  
- first dramatic episode (Black Mirror energy)  

### Stories 11–15: Sarah, Callum, and the Orbiters
- Sarah becomes a main character  
- Callum is her boyfriend  
- Callum’s soft spot emerges  
- Parkers appear  
- Jean’s sister & Jeff’s parent appear  
- Elliot appears late in this block  

### Stories 16–20: The Shift
- Sarah & Callum break up  
- Elliot becomes the new partner  
- Edenshell adapts to the emotional change  
- the family dynamic shifts  
- the house becomes more aware  
- final story: Edenshell does something quietly profound  

This is the arc that makes the booklet feel like a journey, not a collection of shorts.
