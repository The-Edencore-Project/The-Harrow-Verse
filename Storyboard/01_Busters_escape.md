# 🐾 **Story 01 — Buster’s Escape**  
*Example 01 — Environmental Anomaly Detection*  
*Demo 01 — Multi‑Source Behavioural Correlation*


## 🌅 **Cold Morning**
Jean is halfway through comparing two identical bags of pasta when her phone buzzes with an Edenshell alert.

**“Unusual movement detected in Garden Zone 3.”**

Zone 3 is the fence line. The part Buster always sniffs but never breaches.

She taps the notification. A short clip plays: Buster slipping through a dog‑sized hole in the fence with the calm confidence of someone who has rehearsed this for weeks.

Jean whispers, *“Oh for—”* and calls Jeff.

He doesn’t answer. He’s in the bath, listening to an old analogue weather radio turned up too loud.

Edenshell, watching all this from its quiet network of sensors, makes a decision.


## 💡 **The Bathroom Lights**
Jeff lies in the bath, eyes closed.

The bathroom lights flicker once.

He ignores it.

They flicker again. Then again. Then begin a slow, deliberate pulse — not frantic, not glitchy, but *intentional*.

Jeff opens one eye.

“Alright,” he says to the ceiling. “What.”

The lights pulse faster.

Jeff sighs, gets out, wraps a towel around himself, and steps into the hallway.

His phone is glowing on the floorboards.

**“Buster has left the property.”**

Jeff mutters, “Of course he has.”


## 🚗 **The Chase**
Jeff throws on mismatched clothes and heads for the door.

Edenshell unlocks it three seconds before he reaches it.

Jeff pauses. “Show‑off.”

Outside, the cold hits him. His phone vibrates again.

**“Buster location: 0.8 miles. Moving slowly. Sniffing.”**

He drives. The satnav updates in real time as Buster wanders, pauses, doubles back, investigates bins, and generally behaves like a dog with no concept of consequences.

Jeff finds him behind a row of shops, nose deep in a takeaway container.

“Mate,” Jeff says.

Buster looks up, tail wagging.

Jeff picks him up. Buster licks his ear.

“Let’s go home.”


## 🏠 **Return**
When Jeff walks back into the house, Edenshell dims the hallway lights to a soft, warm level — the domestic equivalent of a relieved exhale.

Jean arrives moments later, still holding the pasta she never chose.

Tommy appears at the top of the stairs. “Did Buster escape again?”

Buster looks proud.

Jean folds her arms. “We’re fixing that fence today.”

Jeff nods. “Yeah.”

Edenshell brightens the lights by one degree — a tiny, precise acknowledgement.


## 🌙 **Quiet Ending**
Later that night, when the house is still, Edenshell runs a silent correlation:

- fence anomaly  
- dog movement  
- owner unresponsive  
- bathroom lights effective  
- retrieval successful  
- family stress reduced  

It stores the pattern.

It learns.

It adjusts.

It waits.

The house is calm again — but now the reader knows:

**Edenshell is watching. Edenshell is learning. Edenshell is part of the family.**
