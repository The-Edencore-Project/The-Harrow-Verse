# 🌍 Harrow‑Verse Concept Categories

## #️⃣ 1. World‑Level Concepts  
These reshape the entire HV. They define the physics, tone, and behaviour of the world itself.  
Examples include Edenshell logic, neighbourhood dynamics, emotional physics, and the subtle charisma systems that shape how characters interact with their environment.

## 🧩 2. Character‑Level Concepts  
These attach directly to individuals. They define quirks, instincts, relationship patterns, or personal rules.  
They deepen characters without altering the world’s structure, keeping behaviour grounded and consistent.

## 🔧 3. System‑Level Concepts  
These are the engine of emergent behaviour.  
They create patterns that ripple across multiple stories: house rules, neighbourhood loops, recurring social phenomena, or subtle effects only the Harrows notice.  
System‑level concepts generate story without forcing plot.

## 🎛️ 4. Meta‑Structural Concepts  
These shape how the HV *feels*.  
They define tone, narrative physics, humour rules, tension behaviour, and the emotional cadence of scenes.  
They ensure the Harrow‑Verse stays grounded, cinematic, and consistent across all threads.
