# 🌞 Harrowverse — Master Overview / Index

## 🌐 1. Core Worldbuilding
The Harrowverse is a warm, near‑future suburban universe centred on the Harrow family and their home’s cognitive OS, **Edenshell**. The world blends emotional intelligence, domestic chaos, gentle drama, and realistic IoT behaviour. Every character is lovable, every conflict is human, and the house itself behaves like a living organism. The tone is sunny, aspirational, and emotionally safe: no cruelty, no grime, no bleakness. Technology mirrors humanity, and the home reacts to mood, tension, weather, and routine. The thematic engine drives everything: escapism without denial, tech as a mirror, “nowt so queer as folk,” and love as the default state.

## 🏡 2. Characters
- **The Harrow Family:** Jeff (tense but loving dad), Jean (warm, organised mum), Sarah (bright, emotional uni student), Tommy (chaotic, curious), Buster (the dog).  
- **Extended Family:** Jeff’s mum — sharp, warm, eccentric, never cruel.  
- **Robotic Characters:** Astro (humanoid helper), K9‑Maximus (robot dog).  
- **Edenshell:** The house’s OS — observant, emotionally attuned, occasionally overwhelmed, always trying to help.  
- **Neighbourhood NPCs:** Friendly couple next door, mail carrier, pool guy, gardener, HOA lady, café barista, school bus driver.

## 🏠 3. Technology & IoT
- **IoT Foundation Map:** Room‑by‑room sensors, climate control, lighting, security, pool systems, garden automation, personal devices, wearables.  
- **Edenshell Technical Modes:** Emergency, Weather, Communication, Conflict Resolution, Social, Night, Morning, Learning, Privacy, Maintenance, Guest, Protective.  
- **Emotional‑Intelligence Model:** Mood inference, tension detection, behavioural prediction, de‑escalation strategies.  
- **Device Negotiation Layer:** Vendor‑mixed IoT unified under Edenshell’s translation and coordination.

## 🏘️ 4. Setting
- **The Harrow House:** Bright, open, aspirational; pool, garden, garage, IoT hotspots, robot stations.  
- **Neighbourhood:** Leafy, sunny, friendly suburb with joggers, dog walkers, community events.  
- **Town:** Café, school, university, park, shopping strip, community centre, local services.

## 🎬 5. Influences
- **Primary:** Breaking Bad (craft), The Cosbys (warmth), Married… with Children (chaotic humour), American Dream (aspirational suburbia).  
- **Secondary:** Modern Family, Malcolm in the Middle, Schitt’s Creek, Upload, Humans, Ted Lasso.
