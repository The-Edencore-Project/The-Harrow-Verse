# Harrowverse

The Harrowverse is a near‑future family drama‑comedy set in a warm, leafy American suburb. It follows the Harrow family and their home’s intelligent operating system, Edenshell, as they navigate daily life, emotional chaos, and the quirks of a highly connected smart home.

## Core Principles
- Warm, sunny, emotionally safe tone  
- Lovable characters; no cruelty or bleakness  
- Near‑future realism (no sci‑fi magic)  
- Technology as a mirror for human behaviour  
- Family‑driven stories with humour and heart  

## Key Components
- **Characters:** The Harrow family, extended family, neighbours, robots, and pets  
- **Technology:** Edenshell OS, IoT infrastructure, technical modes, emotional inference  
- **Setting:** The Harrow house, neighbourhood, town, community NPCs  
- **Thematic Engine:** Escapism without denial, house‑as‑organism, love as default state  

## Story Pipeline
1. Dan writes synopses  
2. Mico expands them into full Harrowverse episodes  
3. Tone, humour, IoT logic, and emotional intelligence remain consistent
