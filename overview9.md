# 🌞 Harrowverse — Master Overview / Index

## 🌐 Core Worldbuilding
The Harrowverse is a warm, near‑future suburban universe centred on the Harrow family and their home’s cognitive operating system, Edenshell. Stories blend emotional intelligence, domestic chaos, gentle drama, and realistic IoT behaviour.

The tone stays sunny, aspirational, and emotionally safe, avoiding cruelty or bleakness. Technology mirrors human behaviour, revealing emotion through automation. “Nowt so queer as folk” captures unpredictable family dynamics and comedic misunderstandings.

Love as the default state keeps everything grounded and warm. These principles create a world that feels lived‑in, hopeful, and emotionally resonant, supporting stories that balance humour, heart, and grounded near‑future realism.

## 🏡 Characters
The Harrow family forms the emotional centre of the universe. Jeff is a tense but loving father navigating responsibility, work pressure, and a smart home that sometimes knows him too well.

Jean is warm, organised, and emotionally intelligent, grounding the family with humour and patience. Sarah is expressive, dramatic, and bright, balancing university life with emotional turbulence and the challenges of growing independence.

Tommy is chaotic, curious, and inventive, often triggering Edenshell’s protective or conflict‑resolution modes. Buster the dog adds warmth, mischief, and comedic timing, anchoring the household with charm and loyal energy.

Extended family includes Jeff’s mum, a sharp, eccentric, and deeply loving presence who never crosses into cruelty. Robotic characters include Astro, a polite humanoid helper, and K9‑Maximus, a loyal robot dog.

Neighbourhood NPCs include the couple next door, the mail carrier, the gardener, the pool technician, the HOA lady, and the café barista. These characters create a living community supporting humour, warmth, and story variety.

## 🏠 Technology and IoT
Edenshell is the cognitive OS binding the Harrowverse together. It interprets emotional cues, coordinates IoT devices, manages routines, and reacts to tension, weather, emergencies, and social events.

Its behaviour is realistic, grounded, and never magical. The IoT foundation includes climate control, lighting, security, pool systems, garden automation, personal wearables, and room‑specific sensors across the entire home.

Edenshell’s modes include Emergency, Weather, Communication, Conflict Resolution, Social, Night, Morning, Learning, Privacy, Maintenance, Guest, and Protective. Each mode shapes behaviour, tone, and environmental response.

The emotional‑intelligence model detects mood shifts, misunderstandings, and stress patterns, responding with lighting changes, audio cues, or gentle interventions. The device negotiation layer unifies mixed vendors seamlessly.

## 🏘️ Setting
The Harrow house is bright, open, and aspirational, with a pool, garden, garage workspace, and dedicated robot stations. The neighbourhood is leafy, sunny, and friendly, filled with joggers and dog walkers.

The town includes a café, school, university, park, shopping strip, and community centre. These locations support emotional beats, comedic moments, and IoT‑driven chaos, giving the Harrowverse a lively suburban backdrop.

## 🎬 Influences
Primary influences include Breaking Bad for craft, The Cosbys for warmth, Married… with Children for chaotic humour, and the American Dream for aspirational suburbia.

Secondary influences include Modern Family, Malcolm in the Middle, Schitt’s Creek, Upload, Humans, and Ted Lasso. These shape tone, pacing, emotional beats, and comedic rhythm across the entire universe.

## 🧩 Story Infrastructure
The story pipeline is simple: Dan writes synopses, and Mico expands them into full Harrowverse episodes with humour, structure, and IoT logic. The tone bible ensures warmth and grounded realism.

The drama menu includes storms, parties, tech mishaps, neighbour chaos, emotional spikes, dog escapes, and robot confusion. Episodes follow a structure: cold open, rising chaos, Edenshell misread, emotional beat, resolution, and comedic tag.
