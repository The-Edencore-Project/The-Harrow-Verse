# 🌞 Harrowverse — Master Overview / Index

## 🌐 Core Worldbuilding
The Harrowverse is a warm, near‑future suburban universe centred on the Harrow family and their home’s cognitive operating system, Edenshell. The world blends emotional intelligence, domestic chaos, gentle drama, and realistic IoT behaviour. Every character is lovable, every conflict is human, and the house behaves like a living organism through sensors, routines, and emotional inference. The tone stays sunny, aspirational, and emotionally safe, avoiding cruelty, bleakness, or cynicism entirely.

The thematic engine drives every story with four guiding principles shaping tone and structure. Escapism without denial keeps the world grounded yet comforting. Technology mirrors human behaviour, revealing emotion through automation. “Nowt so queer as folk” captures unpredictable family dynamics. Love as the default state ensures warmth even during chaos. These principles create a world that feels lived‑in, hopeful, and emotionally resonant.

## 🏡 Characters
The Harrow family forms the emotional centre of the universe. Jeff is a tense but loving father navigating responsibility, work pressure, and a smart home that sometimes knows him too well. Jean is warm, organised, and emotionally intelligent, grounding the family with humour and patience. Sarah is expressive, dramatic, and bright, balancing university life with emotional turbulence. Tommy is chaotic, curious, and inventive, often triggering Edenshell’s protective or conflict‑resolution modes. Buster the dog adds warmth, mischief, and comedic timing.

Extended family includes Jeff’s mum, a sharp, eccentric, and deeply loving presence who never crosses into cruelty. Robotic characters include Astro, a humanoid helper with polite efficiency, and K9‑Maximus, a loyal robot dog with protective instincts. Neighbourhood NPCs populate the world with friendly recurring faces: the couple next door, the mail carrier, the gardener, the pool technician, the HOA lady, and the café barista. These characters create a living, breathing community.

## 🏠 Technology and IoT
Edenshell is the cognitive OS binding the Harrowverse together. It interprets emotional cues, coordinates IoT devices, manages routines, and reacts to tension, weather, emergencies, and social events. Its behaviour is realistic, grounded, and never magical. The IoT foundation includes climate control, lighting, security, pool systems, garden automation, personal wearables, and room‑specific sensors. Edenshell’s technical modes include Emergency, Weather, Communication, Conflict Resolution, Social, Night, Morning, Learning, Privacy, Maintenance, Guest, and Protective.

The emotional‑intelligence model allows Edenshell to detect mood shifts, misunderstandings, and stress patterns, responding with lighting changes, audio cues, or gentle interventions. The device negotiation layer ensures compatibility across mixed vendors, allowing Edenshell to unify the home’s technology seamlessly. This creates a believable near‑future environment where technology supports storytelling without overshadowing human emotion.

## 🏘️ Setting
The Harrow house is bright, open, and aspirational, with a pool, garden, garage workspace, and dedicated robot stations. The neighbourhood is leafy, sunny, and friendly, filled with joggers, dog walkers, and community events. The town includes a café, school, university, park, shopping strip, and community centre, providing natural story locations that support emotional and comedic beats. The setting reinforces warmth and optimism.

## 🎬 Influences
Primary influences include Breaking Bad for craft, The Cosbys for warmth, Married… with Children for chaotic humour, and the American Dream for aspirational suburbia. Secondary influences include Modern Family, Malcolm in the Middle, Schitt’s Creek, Upload, Humans, and Ted Lasso. These influences shape tone, pacing, emotional beats, and comedic rhythm, giving the Harrowverse a familiar yet distinct identity.

## 🧩 Story Infrastructure
The story pipeline is simple: Dan writes synopses, and Mico expands them into full Harrowverse episodes with humour, structure, and IoT logic. The tone bible ensures warmth, humanity, grounded realism, and emotional intelligence. The drama menu includes storms, parties, tech mishaps, neighbour chaos, emotional spikes, dog escapes, and robot confusion. Episodes follow a consistent structure: cold open, rising chaos, Edenshell misread, emotional beat, resolution, and comedic tag.
