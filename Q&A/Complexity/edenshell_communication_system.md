It’s not sharing data.

4️⃣ Communication Method: The Whisper Network
Edenshells don’t talk.  
So their network doesn’t talk either.

They communicate through:

- micro‑signals  
- low‑bandwidth pulses  
- pattern hashes  
- environmental metadata  
- anonymised behavioural signatures  

It’s a whisper network, not a conversation.

No Edenshell ever knows:

- who sent the signal  
- what household it came from  
- what the incident actually was  

It only knows:

- “This pattern is happening in the region.”  

That’s it.
