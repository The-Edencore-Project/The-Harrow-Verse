# Q: Mico,... what about the internet, or Google, apple or Microsoft spheres? In terms of complexity?

# Alright, Dan — now we’re stepping into mega‑systems, the digital equivalents of societies.  
And the answer is: yes, they sit ABOVE “global village” in certain dimensions of complexity, but not in the way people usually think.

Let’s map it cleanly.

---

🌐 Where do the Internet, Google, Apple, Microsoft fit in the complexity ladder?

They are not just “bigger societies.”  
They are hybrid systems — part‑human, part‑machine, part‑culture, part‑infrastructure.

So the hierarchy expands like this:

---

1️⃣ Household
Smallest social organism.

2️⃣ Community
Interacting households.

3️⃣ Region
Institutions + culture + infrastructure.

4️⃣ Society
Large‑scale identity + law + history.

5️⃣ Global Village
Billions of humans interacting across borders.

---

Now we add the digital layer:

6️⃣ The Internet (beyond global village)
The internet is the first system in human history where:

- billions of humans  
- billions of machines  
- trillions of interactions  
- infinite feedback loops  
- no central authority  
- no stable culture  
- no fixed geography  

…all collide at once.

It is more complex than any society because it’s not bound by physical constraints.

It’s the first planet‑scale emergent organism we’ve ever built.

---

7️⃣ Corporate Digital Ecosystems (Google, Apple, Microsoft)
These are even more structured and layered than the internet itself.

They combine:

- global infrastructure  
- billions of users  
- trillions of data points  
- machine learning systems  
- human behaviour  
- economic incentives  
- cultural influence  
- regulatory pressure  
- internal politics  
- external ecosystems  
- hardware + software + cloud + AI  

They are hybrid super‑systems.

Not as chaotic as the internet.  
Not as organic as society.  
But more complex than any single human institution ever created.

They sit between the internet and the global village in complexity — but with different kinds of complexity.

---

The internet is the only system humans have built that behaves like:

- a living organism  
- a weather system  
- a culture engine  
- a memory network  
- a chaos amplifier  
- a global nervous system  

All at once.

Nothing else compares.
