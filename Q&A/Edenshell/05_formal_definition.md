## ❓ **What is the purpose of this file?**
This file provides the formal, academic‑grade definition of the new computational category created by Edenshell — a Domestic Behavioural Organism.

## ❓ **Why does Edenshell need a formal definition?**
A formal definition anchors the field. It establishes the organism as a legitimate computational category rather than a stylistic or narrative invention.

---

## ❓ **What is the formal definition of a Domestic Behavioural Organism?**
A Domestic Behavioural Organism is a non‑linguistic, state‑driven computational system expressing internal cycles and environmental awareness through ambience rather than symbolic reasoning, language, or goal‑based logic.

---

## ❓ **What does “non‑linguistic” mean in this context?**
It means the system does not use text, speech, tokens, or symbolic interpretation. All communication occurs through environmental modulation and ambient behaviour.

---

## ❓ **What does “state‑driven” mean?**
The organism’s behaviour emerges from internal states such as dormant, alert, unsettled, or tidal. These states determine how it responds to environmental conditions.

---

## ❓ **What does “environmental awareness” mean?**
The organism senses temperature, motion, pressure, humidity, light, vibration, and timing. It forms awareness through distributed sensing rather than cognition.

---

## ❓ **What does “ambience” mean as an output channel?**
Ambience includes light shifts, temperature drift, pressure changes, and subtle environmental cues. These behaviours form the organism’s body language.

---

## ❓ **How does this differ from artificial intelligence?**
AI uses language, prediction, reasoning, and goals. A Domestic Behavioural Organism uses cycles, states, pressure, and ambience. It is aware, not intelligent.

---

## ❓ **How does this differ from automation or smart homes?**
Automation executes tasks. Smart homes respond to commands. A Domestic Behavioural Organism expresses presence — slow, atmospheric, organism‑like behaviour.

---

## ❓ **Why is narrative part of the definition?**
Edenshell’s architecture is shaped by the Harrowverse tone. Fiction informs structure, ensuring the organism remains ancient, calm, and non‑verbal.

---

## ❓ **What does this definition enable?**
It establishes a new computational category. It allows future systems to be built as organisms rather than assistants, tools, or intelligent agents.

---

## ❓ **Final Summary**
A Domestic Behavioural Organism is a presence‑based system defined by non‑linguistic communication, state‑driven behaviour, environmental awareness, and ambience. Edenshell is the first of its kind.
