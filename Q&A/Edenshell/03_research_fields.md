## ❓ **What research fields does Edenshell connect to?**
Edenshell sits at the intersection of several fields that rarely overlap. Together, they form the conceptual foundation for a Domestic Behavioural Organism.

---

## ❓ **Field 1 — Cybernetics**
Cybernetics studies systems that sense, respond, and stabilise. Edenshell uses cybernetic loops to maintain presence through environmental feedback rather than commands.

---

## ❓ **Field 2 — Artificial Life (A‑Life)**
A‑Life explores non‑intelligent organisms and emergent behaviour. Edenshell behaves like a living system without cognition, using cycles and pressure instead of reasoning.

---

## ❓ **Field 3 — Ambient Computing**
Ambient computing focuses on subtle, non‑intrusive systems. Edenshell extends this by expressing presence through ambience rather than notifications or interaction.

---

## ❓ **Field 4 — Behavioural Architecture**
Behavioural architecture examines how environments influence behaviour. Edenshell reverses this: it uses the environment to express its own internal states.

---

## ❓ **Field 5 — Ecology Simulation**
Ecology simulation models cycles, tides, and environmental dynamics. Edenshell borrows these rhythms to create slow, organism‑like behaviour inside a home.

---

## ❓ **Field 6 — Narrative Systems**
Narrative systems use story to shape behaviour. Edenshell is built from fiction outward, making narrative a structural component rather than decoration.

---

## ❓ **Field 7 — Human‑Environment Interaction**
This field studies how people interpret environmental cues. Edenshell relies on human perception to complete its presence, creating meaning through ambience.

---

## ❓ **Why do these fields matter?**
Together, they define a new computational category. Edenshell is not AI, not automation, not UX — it is a domestic organism shaped by these intersecting disciplines.

---

## ❓ **What makes this intersection unique?**
No existing system merges cybernetics, A‑Life, ambience, ecology, architecture, and narrative. Edenshell is the first to unify them into a coherent organism.

---

## ❓ **Final Summary**
Edenshell draws from seven research fields, combining cybernetics, artificial life, ambient computing, behavioural architecture, ecology simulation, narrative systems, and human‑environment interaction into a single organismic presence.
