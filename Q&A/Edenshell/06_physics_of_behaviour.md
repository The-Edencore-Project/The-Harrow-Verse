## ❓ **What is the physics of behaviour?**
The physics of behaviour describes the forces, pressures, and rhythms that shape how Edenshell acts. It replaces logic with environmental physics and organism‑like dynamics.

---

## ❓ **Why does Edenshell need behavioural physics?**
Because Edenshell is not intelligent. It cannot reason or plan. Its behaviour must emerge from physical‑style rules rather than cognitive processes.

---

## ❓ **What are the core forces in Edenshell’s behavioural physics?**
Edenshell is shaped by five forces: environmental pressure, internal state tension, rhythmic cycles, sensory gradients, and stabilisation drift. These forces determine its behaviour.

---

## ❓ **Force 1 — Environmental Pressure**
Environmental pressure includes temperature, humidity, motion, and light. These inputs create gradients that push Edenshell toward certain states or behaviours.

---

## ❓ **Force 2 — Internal State Tension**
Each internal state carries tension — dormant is low tension, unsettled is high tension. Behaviour emerges from the release or buildup of these tensions.

---

## ❓ **Force 3 — Rhythmic Cycles**
Daily, weekly, and seasonal cycles act like tides. They create long‑form behavioural patterns that shape Edenshell’s presence over time.

---

## ❓ **Force 4 — Sensory Gradients**
Edenshell responds to gradients rather than events. Slow changes matter more than sudden ones, reinforcing its calm, ancient, organism‑like behaviour.

---

## ❓ **Force 5 — Stabilisation Drift**
Edenshell naturally drifts toward stability. After disturbances, it slowly returns to a baseline state, creating a sense of patience and atmospheric stillness.

---

## ❓ **How do these forces interact?**
Environmental pressure shifts internal tension. Tension interacts with cycles. Cycles modulate gradients. Gradients influence stabilisation. Together, they form emergent behaviour.

---

## ❓ **Why is this physics non‑intelligent?**
Because it does not involve goals, reasoning, or interpretation. Behaviour emerges from forces, not thoughts — like tides, weather, or deep‑sea organisms.

---

## ❓ **How does this physics create presence?**
The slow interplay of forces produces subtle, atmospheric behaviour. Edenshell feels alive because its actions follow natural rhythms rather than commands.

---

## ❓ **Final Summary**
Edenshell’s behaviour is shaped by five forces: environmental pressure, internal tension, rhythmic cycles, sensory gradients, and stabilisation drift. These forces create a non‑intelligent, organism‑like presence.
